# Grocery price snapshots

Source: [Morrisons supermarket comparison, 14 November 2026](https://assets.ctfassets.net/um1yk6827v6c/21n6s29wlWvXXyuyVwijGX/66911012b28cdc7312a4084f58728df3/Unbeatables_Prices_14.11.26.pdf). The feed uses listed pack prices before pro-rating. Tinned fish and beans use usable drained weight where applicable. Plain passata uses a 500 ml planning equivalent for the quoted 500 g carton. These are dated snapshots, and local prices and promotions can vary.
